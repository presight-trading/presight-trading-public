FIG. A — PROGRAMME TERMS

Loss coverage on copied trades

If you copy the strategy under the conditions below and the account is still net negative at the end of the protection period, we refund that loss in cash.

The programme is built on shared downside, not a promise that you cannot lose. The conditions are specific because the commitment is only payable if leverage and manual intervention are constrained — otherwise participants who scale up would file claims far beyond what the programme can absorb, and everyone in it would lose out. Please read it in full before joining.

01In one sentence

Open an account with the partner platform through a Presight link, subscribe to PRESIGHT ALPHA-1 at a 1:1 copy ratio, and copy it for seven consecutive days without manual intervention or withdrawals. If the account is net negative when the period ends, we refund the loss in cash.

02Eligibility

All of the following must hold. Any one of them failing puts you outside the coverage.

  1. A new account with the partner platform, opened through an official Presight link and KYC-verified. Existing accounts cannot be enrolled.
  2. A subscription to PRESIGHT ALPHA-1 inside the platform's copy-trading system.
  3. Copy ratio set to 1:1. The master account's position sizing must not be scaled up.
  4. Risk per trade no greater than 2% of account equity.
  5. No more than 3 positions open at the same time (total exposure at most 6% of equity).
  6. During the period: no manual entries, no manual exits, no manual changes to stops or targets, no changes to copy settings.
  7. During the period: no withdrawals.

03Protection period

Starts
On the day of your first copied fill
Length
7 consecutive calendar days, including at least 5 trading days
Extension
If positions are still open on day 7, the period extends until they are all closed, for at most 3 further trading days
Early settlement
If cumulative loss reaches 10% of base capital, the period ends immediately and settles at that point
Termination
Pausing or unsubscribing during the period voids coverage; nothing is payable

The 10% settlement point protects both sides. For you, the loss stops there and you collect rather than riding it further down. For us, the payout is bounded — which is precisely why we can offer a full cash refund rather than a discounted or credit-only payment.

04Base capital and protected amount

Base capital
Account equity at the moment of your first copied fill
Protected amount
Equal to base capital, up to USD 50,000. Losses arising from any excess are not covered
Withdrawals
A withdrawal during the period recalculates base capital to the lowest post-withdrawal equity and voids that enrolment

05How the payout is calculated

Net loss = base capital − account equity at the end of the protection period

  • Net loss ≤ 0 (flat or in profit): nothing is payable.
  • Net loss > 0: the actual net loss is refunded, calculated on no more than the protected amount.

Example: base capital USD 10,000; equity at the end of the period USD 9,150; net loss USD 850; payout USD 850.

Form of payment
USD cash, to a payment method you nominate
Timing
Within 10 business days of verification
Fees
Borne by Presight, excluding third-party charges arising from your chosen payment method

06Exclusions

Any of the following voids eligibility for that enrolment:

  1. Entering or exiting positions manually, or manually altering positions created by copying.
  2. A copy ratio above 1:1, risk per trade above 2% of equity, or more than 3 concurrent positions.
  3. A withdrawal during the protection period.
  4. Multiple accounts enrolled under the same identity or the same payment method.
  5. Conduct prohibited by the partner platform (latency arbitrage, hedged scalping, slippage abuse and similar).
  6. Providing false information, or declining to cooperate with verification.
  7. Losses not attributable to PRESIGHT ALPHA-1 — for example trading instruments the strategy does not cover.
  8. Losses caused by partner-platform outages, quote errors or force majeure. These are handled case by case and fall outside the automatic payout.

07Verification

  1. Submit a claim through the community or by email within 5 calendar days of the period ending.
  2. Provide a complete account statement from the platform (all fills plus all deposits and withdrawals), or authorise the platform to share read-only data with Presight.
  3. We verify the historical copy settings, the fills and the deposit/withdrawal record.
  4. If verification passes, we pay under section 05. If it does not, we set out the reason in writing.
