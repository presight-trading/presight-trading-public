FIG. 01 — HEADLINE OFFER

Loss coverage on copied trades

Copy within the rules and we cover the loss. This is not a promise that you cannot lose — it is us sharing the downside of that period once leverage and manual intervention are constrained. The conditions are specific because that is the only way the commitment stays payable.

01

1:1 copy ratio, no scaling up

All you set is the 1:1 ratio. Per-trade risk and aggregate exposure are managed by us at the copy-account level — ALPHA-1 is a quantitative strategy, and holding several positions at once is normal structure, not something you need to configure.

02

Account-level drawdown control

The strategy account has drawdown thresholds; on breach it automatically reduces size or stops opening new positions, and copy accounts follow. The strategy never averages down.

03

Seven days, net loss refunded

Seven consecutive calendar days from your first fill (including five trading days), no manual intervention, no withdrawals. If equity ends below base capital, the shortfall is refunded in USD cash within 10 business days of verification.

⚠ Before you join

Coverage is limited to the full amount of your base capital. Opening trades manually, raising the copy ratio above 1:1, or withdrawing during the period all void eligibility.

FIG. 05 — FAQ

About the strategy and copy trading

How is the loss coverage triggered and calculated?

Base capital is your account equity at the moment of your first copied fill. If equity at the end of the protection period is below base capital, that shortfall is the net loss and is refunded in USD cash within 10 business days of verification. Nothing is owed if you finish flat or in profit. Coverage is limited to the full amount of base capital. Full terms and exclusions →

What do I have to do to qualify?

Four: a new account opened through our link and KYC-verified; copy ratio set to 1:1 (no scaling up); seven consecutive calendar days from your first fill (including five trading days); and no manual intervention and no withdrawals during that window.

Per-trade risk and aggregate exposure are not yours to set — we manage them at the copy-account level. ALPHA-1 is a quantitative strategy and holding several positions at once is normal. The strategy account has drawdown thresholds that cut size or halt new entries on breach, and it never averages down.

Do I have to use the specified platform?

Yes. Copy trading is a signal-sync mechanism inside the platform, and only accounts registered through this site’s link appear on the subscription list. Accounts you already hold elsewhere cannot be connected.

How do you make money? Why is the community free?

Copy-trading revenue comes from partner rebates and a share of strategy performance, scaling with copied volume. When the strategy earns nothing, neither do we — our incentives point the same way as yours. The community is an acquisition channel and is not billed separately.

Is the track record filtered?

The fills on this page come straight from the strategy’s live account with no filtering, and losing trades are listed alongside the rest. Past performance does not indicate future results, and every strategy has drawdown periods.
